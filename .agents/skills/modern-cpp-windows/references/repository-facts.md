| `RedPrism` | The `JobScheduler` worker runner catches only `std::bad_alloc` and translates it to a failed job with `E_OUTOFMEMORY`. Other allocation failures stay fatal except at explicit `noexcept` construction/admission boundaries. | `AGENTS.md` | Behaviour of this product; listed in Differences To Converge. |

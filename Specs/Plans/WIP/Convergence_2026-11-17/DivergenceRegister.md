# Divergence register

Companion to [the convergence plan](../Convergence_2026-11-17.md). It lists every known option, rule, or file that is not the same in all repositories, with one common target each. The owner's rule decides every row: at the end, all projects are as close as possible. A difference survives only when the product requires it, and then the reason is written in the row.

**Status at the 19 November implementation handoff.** The current PR queue, pinned source identities and D21 receipts are in the [plan](../Convergence_2026-11-17.md#implementation-queue) and its [test ledger](DeferredTests.md). Open PRs and unmerged local branches are implementation inputs. A row with no remaining owners and a Done or Merged disposition records completed work. Rows naming other owners remain open for them. For a new pin wave, compare the target again at the receiving repository's current main.

**Baseline sources (17 November).** Four file-level inventories of the six `main` branches at the plan's baseline commits (build and toolchain, script entry points, guidance and documentation, CI and project configuration), the `Differences To Converge` tables of the shared skills, and spot checks of the high-impact rows. Effective MSBuild evaluation was not run; property rows come from reading the files. Correct a row here when a repository proves it wrong.

**Review of 2026-11-17.** A second pass on the Windows host re-read the build, formatting, guidance, and local-skill files of all six repositories, hashed the files claimed identical, and checked GitHub. It corrected A-01, A-02, A-10, A-12, C-02, C-03, D-01, D-02, F-04, and G-02, and added A-14 to A-18, B-13, C-10 to C-19, D-05, D-06, E-11 to E-13, G-11, G-12, and H-14 to H-16.

**Abbreviations.** RS `RedSalamander`, Xe `RedXe`, Pr `RedPrism`, Ti `RedTilde`, Mo `RedMonitor`, Dx `DxUi`, Home `RedHome`.

**Columns.** Today describes the cited repository state. Target gives the common behavior. Changes names the remaining owners, and In names the implementation phase or the API 6 handoff. Decisions D22–D25 were confirmed by the owner on 19 November.
